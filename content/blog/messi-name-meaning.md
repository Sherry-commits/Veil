## The Origin of the Name Lionel

The name *Lionel* began in medieval Europe as a diminutive of *Leo*, the Latin word for **lion**. In the old romances and courtly tales, Lionel was the name given to brave knights — figures of courage, loyalty, and the kind of strength that protects rather than merely overwhelms. To carry the lion''s name was to carry the lion''s nature: a heart that does not flinch, a will that holds the line when others fall back.

In Veil''s reading, a Lionel does not simply happen to be brave. The name sets a direction — toward heart, toward leading from the front, toward the courage that is gentle enough to love and fierce enough to fight for what it loves. It is the name of the one others gather behind when the moment asks for more than talent.

To step back from the world''s stage after losing the man who first believed in you, and to choose Rosario, is not weakness. It is the Fire remembering that it is allowed to warm its own hearth. The name — lion, brave one, loyal to home — was pointing here all along.
